Attention, from the softmax up

Scaled dot-product attention one piece at a time, with a temperature dial you can turn to watch a query make up its mind.

Every transformer layer asks the same question once per token: of everything I have seen so far, what should I copy from? Attention is the answer, and it fits in one line. This placeholder post walks through that line slowly, so the typography, the math and the margin notes have something real to hold.

The one line

Given queries Q∈Rn×dQ \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}Q∈Rn×d, keys K∈Rn×dK \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}K∈Rn×d and values V∈Rn×dvV \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d_v}V∈Rn×dv​, a single head computes

Attention⁡(Q,K,V)=softmax⁡ ⁣(QK⊤d+M)V,\operatorname{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d}} + M\right) V, Attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(d​QK⊤​+M)V,

where MMM is the causal mask: 000 on and below the diagonal, −∞-\infty−∞ above it.1 Row iii of the softmax is a probability distribution over the positions j≤ij \le ij≤i, and the output for token iii is the matching weighted average of value vectors.

The d\sqrt{d}d​ is there for variance. If the entries of qqq and kkk are independent with zero mean and unit variance, then

Var⁡(q⊤k)=∑t=1dVar⁡(qtkt)=d,\operatorname{Var}(q^\top k) = \sum_{t=1}^{d} \operatorname{Var}(q_t k_t) = d, Var(q⊤k)=t=1∑d​Var(qt​kt​)=d,

so without the scale the logits grow with width and the softmax saturates into a one-hot vector, which has almost no gradient.

Temperature, by hand

Dividing by d\sqrt{d}d​ is a fixed temperature. Turning it by hand shows what it does. Below is one query, the token was, scoring the seven tokens before it. Low temperature sharpens the distribution toward the highest score; high temperature flattens it toward uniform.

The entropy readout gives the same picture as a number. At τ→0\tau \to 0τ→0 it falls to zero bits (one token gets everything); as τ→∞\tau \to \inftyτ→∞ it approaches log⁡27≈2.81\log_2 7 \approx 2.81log2​7≈2.81 bits, the uniform distribution over seven visible tokens.
