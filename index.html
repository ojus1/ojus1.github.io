About

I'm Surya Kant Sahu. I'm impatient with slow programs, so I tend to end up with a pile of second-hand machines, a local network and NCCL, and a job that runs faster than it did yesterday.

I'm interested in transformers, learning theory, reinforcement learning and machine learning for audio and music. I work mostly in Python and C++, with PyTorch, ONNX, Numba and Cython.
